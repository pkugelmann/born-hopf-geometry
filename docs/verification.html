Status & provenance

What this release actually establishes

Status is scoped, not binary. The supplied audit migration record enumerates 250 historical issue IDs, including 73 unadjudicated and 33 earlier source-repaired items still pending stronger verification at that snapshot. Later paper revisions and targeted checks are not thereby re-certified in full. The full audit ledgers, source anchors and evidence requirements control the status of each result.
A / Source correction

Statement repaired

A formula, theorem premise or reference may be corrected in the current source without proving its stronger observable implication.

B / Bounded check

Calculation replayed

A finite test or exact computation supports only its declared domain and does not automatically certify a universal theorem.

C / Physical bridge

Observable established?

The extra source, action, existence, pole or measurement hypotheses still need explicit closure where named.
