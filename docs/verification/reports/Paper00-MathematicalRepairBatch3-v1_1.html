Result and boundaries

Three current manuscripts were revised and compiled to PDF, and seven named audit defects were repaired in their source theorem statements. Nine additional previously corrected claims were examined using bounded symbolic, type, or finite-dimensional counterexample checks. No whole-paper independent E3/E4 audit was carried out; no assumption about the existence of a physical observable is silently discharged.

The 26 checks in Paper00-MathematicalRepairBatch3Verification-v1_1.json pass. Major remaining proof boundaries

  1. GP3 whole-candidate and physical instantiation: The established m_p=1, H_1, finite momentum carrier and exact local geometric results remain established. Whether the whole-candidate comparator and specified physical matter occurrence are provided is a distinct owner obligation, not reopened or manufactured by this batch.
  2. Gravity/measure and K16/K48: Exact source-compatible stress response, state selection and occurrence must be demonstrated in the same action/spectral owner, not inferred from a finite identity or historic dressing comparison.
  3. Hadron poles/physical masses: A two-state trial transfer matrix does not guarantee full Hamiltonian pole location without a reducing-sector or full-resolvent/self-energy certificate. The prior VII-B source repair remains conditional.
  4. Continuum/realization: Operator overlap, finite-refinement QFT4 defects, Wick/Weyl regularity and classical Poisson dynamics require actual owners, uniform bounds and suitable limit topology.
  5. Missing exact witness data: Original VII-D audit issues describing numerical/algorithmic 864×864 inertia witness and chiral-/incidence checker artifacts are not closed solely by a manuscript's assertion of their existence; replay requires the executable finite inputs.
  6. Untouched audits: Do not promote the 76 unadjudicated and 33 source-repaired still-pending records; 40 historically resolved statuses have not been independently rechecked in this run.
