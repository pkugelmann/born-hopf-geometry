About this archive

Born–Hopf Geometry

An open-to-read archive of a research program authored by Peter Kugelmann.

Purpose

This site provides a stable point of entry to versioned mathematical and physical manuscripts, their technical source files and a distinct verification layer. It is intended for readers who want to inspect definitions, hypotheses, proofs and the proposed relationships to observables.

Authorship and citation

The manuscript PDFs and LaTeX identify Peter Kugelmann as author. When citing a specific result, reference the relevant paper, its version and—where applicable—its theorem number and conditions. A repository-level CITATION.cff is supplied for convenience.

Use and licensing

No third-party reuse license has been inferred or invented. Unless the author adds an explicit license, the manuscripts and other protected content remain subject to applicable copyright. Public read access is not the same as permission to redistribute or modify.

Status transparency

The project contains conditional mathematical statements, source-specific proof repairs, finite computational checks and unresolved bridges to physical observables. It should not be presented as having passed an independent, whole-framework physics or mathematical verification.

Versions

Current source snapshot: PapersMinimal v3.058, ProofChainAudits v1.79, DAGAtlas v1.215 and S3StructureNetwork v1.41, supplied on 20 November 2026. The website package is labeled Paper00-GitHubPage-v1_1.
