# What changed in the current framework?

This site currently reflects **PapersMinimal v3.077**, with **ProofChainAudits v1.83**, **DAG Atlas v1.223**, and **S³ Structure Network v1.49**. The manuscript update is dated 20 November 2026. The current archive contains **31 active papers** across seven volumes.

The important point is not simply that files have newer version numbers. In the latest source, several finite constructions became more explicit. They may strengthen the geometric route to observables, but their independently audited scope and their physical occurrence still have to be distinguished.

## Five-core geometry and transition direction

In [Paper I–B](paper.html?paper=Paper1B), the revised source develops the resistance and comparison structure of a five-core network, its reversible Laplacian, and the relationship to oriented input/output ports. The source-cyclic Schur return is an exact finite comparison construction. **A reversible comparison kernel is not by itself a physical decay arrow.** The observed direction and strength of a decay require the appropriate dynamics and source.

## Localization and finite measurement outcomes

[Paper II–C](paper.html?paper=Paper2C) now includes a reduction of compact finite-dimensional continuous-outcome POVMs to finite positive representatives preserving the relevant effect-span rank, together with intrinsic-position rank thresholds and a fixed-radius approximation limit. This extends a *mathematical* localization constraint; it does not establish that a particular detector or measurement process occurs physically.

## Dynamics and reference subtraction

[Paper III–F](paper.html?paper=Paper3F) examines the fixed-background off-diagonal nonmetric matter current in an adopted reference-subtracted action family. Under the stated source conditions, the nonmetric matter vertices and transition matrix elements are insensitive to that subtraction. This is a useful consistency result **within the chosen action family**; it does not settle metric variations or the selection of the physical action.

## Hadrons and the missing physical current

The most extensive recent additions are in [Paper VII–A](paper.html?paper=Paper7A) and [Paper VII–B](paper.html?paper=Paper7B). They cover source-cyclic compression, Schur vertices, Ward conditions, Hurwitz baryon structures, root-normalized diagnostics, a 56-state decuplet–octet tensor, and conditional axial transition strengths and bounds.

The distinction is crucial: the current source provides **geometric and operator comparison strengths**, not a table of unconditional decay widths or lifetimes. It explicitly retains the need for the physically selected Gauss-reduced source/current, the complete mixed-current moments, actual poles, open-channel absorptive continuation, and a common absolute normalization. Neither mass differences alone nor scalar overlap are promoted into a decay amplitude.
